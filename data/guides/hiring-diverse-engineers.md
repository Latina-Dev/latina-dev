These takeaways come from conversations I've had with recruiters and startup teams about hiring more diverse software engineers.

## Takeaways

- It's great when the internal team, not just recruiting, feels responsible for helping increase diversity and inclusion.
- Double-check that your job description isn't using exclusionary language, using a tool like [Textio](https://textio.com/).
- Intentionally reach out to communities by providing value upfront, since job boards alone may not return a high ROI.
- If you do use job boards, say so on your careers page to back up how vital diversity and inclusion is to the team.
- Reach out to niche, intersectional communities that cater to the audience who would be interested in what your company is doing (for example, [Latinx in Gaming](https://www.latinxingaming.com/)).
- Add a disclaimer on your careers page so potential candidates know the company is self-aware of its racial homogeneity and committed to improving it.
- If there isn't an internal infrastructure or culture to support junior engineers, it probably doesn't make sense to target recent bootcamp grads.
- However, if the bootcamp grads you're looking at have at least two years of experience with almost all the same technologies, they can be a very good fit.
- Show off your supportive culture for parents on your careers page.
- Show off the invisible diversity among your employees through video on your careers page.
